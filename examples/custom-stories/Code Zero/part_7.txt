Day 18: No Exit

The military arrived, but they didn't come to rescue us. They’re firebombing the upper levels. They’re trying to seal the hive inside the mountain. I’m trapped in the sub-levels with three other scientists. One of them is coughing up a thick, black fluid that moves on its own. He says the fluid is singing to him. I’m starting to hear it too. A low, beautiful hum.
