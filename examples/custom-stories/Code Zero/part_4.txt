Day 9: The Breach

Aris died at 03:00. We prepared for the autopsy, but when the scalpel touched his chest, his ribs swung outward like a trap. He wasn't dead. His nervous system had been completely replaced by the organism. He killed the two orderlies before we could trigger the emergency locks. He didn't eat them—he just bit them once and moved to the next. He's "planting seeds."
