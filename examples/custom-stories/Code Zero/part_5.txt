Day 12: Outbreak

The facility is in lockdown, but it’s too late. The orderlies "woke up" an hour ago. They work with a collective intelligence I can't explain. They cut the power to the cooling systems. The organism thrives in the heat. I can hear them in the vents—not crawling, but slithering. It sounds like a thousand wet snakes. The air smells like ozone and rotting meat.
