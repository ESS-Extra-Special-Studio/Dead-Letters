Day 30: The Last Message

My name is Clara. My dad is gone. He’s still in the shack, but he doesn't look like Dad. He looks like the sun. I’m in the radio room, but the wires are cut. I can see the mainland from here. It’s burning. All of it. I have the rebar now. Dad said it was for protection, but I think I’m going to use it to go find him. I don't want to be the last one who remembers the birds.
