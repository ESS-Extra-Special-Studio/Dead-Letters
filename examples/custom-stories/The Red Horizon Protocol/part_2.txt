Day 3: The Breach

Henderson came back. He didn't knock; he just put his head through the glass of the front door. He didn't even use his hands. I had to use the garden shears. Clara saw it. God, she saw all of it. We’re taking the SUV and heading North. They say the cold slows the spread. If the driveway is empty, we’ve already left. Check the glovebox for the spare shells.
