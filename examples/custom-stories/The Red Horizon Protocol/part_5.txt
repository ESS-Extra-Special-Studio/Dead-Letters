Day 15: The Fever

I was careless. A scavenger jumped us near the loading dock. I fought him off, but he nicked my arm with a shard of glass he'd been using as a knife. He was covered in black veins. I’ve scrubbed the wound with alcohol until I screamed, but the skin around it is already turning that sickly, bruised grey. Clara doesn't know. I’m wearing long sleeves. I have to get her to the Cordon.
