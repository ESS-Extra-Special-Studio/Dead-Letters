Day 24: The Hunger

Can’t... think. The grey is in my neck. Every time Clara speaks, my teeth ache. I want to tell her I love her, but all that comes out is a clicking sound. I’ve sharpened a piece of rebar. I’m going to do it before the sun sets. I left her the last of the crackers and the map to the radio room. Please, if anyone finds her... she’s just a little girl.
