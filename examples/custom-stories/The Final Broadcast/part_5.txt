Day 14: The Hive

I watched the monitors tonight. The crew isn't sleeping in their bunks anymore. They’re all in the main hall, standing in a circle, their arms entwined like vines. They’ve started "weaving." They’re sewing their own skin together with copper wire from the walls. They’re building something in the center. A throne? A cocoon? I can’t stop looking at it. It’s beautiful in a way that makes me want to scream.
