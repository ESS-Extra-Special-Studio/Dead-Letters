Day 17: The First Bite

The medic found me. He didn't use his teeth—he used a surgical saw. He told me he wanted to "see the music" inside my ribs. I shot him, but he didn't fall. He just looked at the hole in his chest and laughed. Green dust puffed out of the wound like smoke. I’m hiding in the radio room now. My left hand is gone. I’ve cauterized the stump with a soldering iron. The pain is the only thing keeping me from joining the circle.
