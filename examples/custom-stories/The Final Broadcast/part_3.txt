Day 7: No More Water

The pipes are clogged. When we opened the main valve to check, we didn't find lime or rust. We found hair. Miles and miles of thick, black hair growing inside the steel pipes. It’s pulsing. We have no water, and the "coughers" are getting aggressive. They don't want medicine; they want to touch you. They want to press their foreheads against yours and just... hum.
