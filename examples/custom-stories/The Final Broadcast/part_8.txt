Day 24: Darkened Halls

I cut the main power. I thought the darkness would stop them, but they don't need eyes. They see the heat of my blood. I’m crawling through the maintenance tunnels, clutching the radio transmitter. My skin is starting to itch, and when I scratch it, the green powder falls out. I’m becoming the very thing I’m running from. I just want to see the sky one last time before the "weaving" is finished.
