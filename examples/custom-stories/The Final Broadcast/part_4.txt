Day 10: Trigger Finger

The commander locked himself in the armory. We heard a single shot at 02:00. When we broke the door down, he was gone, but his uniform was still there, sitting in the chair. It was filled with that green powder and a swarm of black flies. No body. No blood. Just the clothes and a note that said: "It’s louder when you’re dead." I took his sidearm. I think I’ll need it for the medic.
