Day 27: The Horizon

I reached the surface. The world is grey. The trees, the grass, the buildings... everything is covered in that thick, pulsing hair. There are no birds. There is no wind. Just the hum. I saw a group of survivors on the road, but they weren't survivors. They were a bridge. They’d fused their bodies together to span a gap in the highway. Their eyes were open, and they were singing.
