Day 21: Dead Air

The hum is coming through the headphones now. I’ve cut the wires, but I can still hear it. It’s not a sound; it’s a thought. It’s telling me that the world outside is already "woven." That I’m the last loose thread. I can hear them scratching at the door. They aren't trying to break it; they’re trying to "unpick" the lock with their fingernails.
