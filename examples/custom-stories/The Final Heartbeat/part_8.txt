Day 22: The Silence

I tried to pray, but I’ve forgotten the words. My tongue feels too thick for my mouth. Sarah’s jaw has unhinged—it hangs low, dripping a black, viscous fluid. We found a group of survivors in the cellar. They were praying too. It was a beautiful sound, like a dinner bell. I’m writing this with a piece of charcoal because my fingers can't hold a pen anymore.
