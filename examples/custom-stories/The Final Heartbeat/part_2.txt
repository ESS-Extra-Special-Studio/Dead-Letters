Day 3: The Scratching

Something is in the house. It didn't break a window; it came through the vents. It sounds like a wet cloth being dragged across the floor. I'm in the closet with the baseball bat. Sarah is crying, and I have to keep my hand over her mouth so hard I’m afraid I’ll bruise her. The thing outside the door just stopped. It’s sniffing the air. It smells like old copper.
