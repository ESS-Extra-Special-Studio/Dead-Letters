Day 12: The Shedding

I found a pile of skin in the corner of the barn. It was in the shape of a human arm, perfectly intact, like a snake had shed it. Sarah’s hair is falling out in clumps. She doesn't cry anymore. She just sits there and stares at the wall, clicking her tongue against her teeth. Click. Click. Click. It’s the same sound I heard in the vents on Day 3.
