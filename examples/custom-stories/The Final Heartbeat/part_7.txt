Day 19: The Black Veins

I found a mirror. My eyes are gone. There’s just a milky, silver film where my pupils used to be. I can see, but not like before. I see heat. I see the pulse in the necks of the birds in the trees. Sarah is faster than me now. She caught a drifter yesterday. I didn't stop her. I helped her. We’re not a family anymore. We’re a pack.
