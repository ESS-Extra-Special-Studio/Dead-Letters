Day 15: The Hunger

We ran out of canned food. I caught a rabbit, but when I tried to cook it, the smell of the fire made Sarah sick. She didn't want the cooked meat. She wanted it raw. I watched my eight-year-old daughter tear into a bloody carcass with her bare teeth. The worst part isn't the horror—it’s that I was jealous of her. My own stomach is screaming for something warm and wet.
