Day 3: The Lunch Lady

We ran out of snacks in the classroom. I had to sneak down to the kitchen. I found Mrs. Gable, the lunch lady. She was sitting in the walk-in freezer, but the door was open. She had a pile of raw chicken nuggets in her lap, but she wasn't eating them. She was sewing them onto her own skin with a plastic fork. She looked at me and whispered, "The meat must be cold to stay quiet." I took the juice boxes and ran.
