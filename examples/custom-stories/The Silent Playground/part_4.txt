Day 9: Finger Painting

There is no more red paint, but the kids are still painting. They’re using the "thick paint" from their own gums. They’ve covered the walls with murals of a giant, eyeless face. They call it "The Principal." They say The Principal is coming to give us our final grades. I tried to take the brushes away, but they bit me. Their teeth feel like needles. The wounds aren't red; they're bright, glowing blue.
