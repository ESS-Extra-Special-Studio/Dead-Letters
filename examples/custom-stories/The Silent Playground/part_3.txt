Day 6: Hide and Seek

One of the first-graders, Toby, got out during the night. We found him in the gym. He was hanging from the basketball hoop by his shirt collar. He wasn't dead, but his neck had stretched like taffy. He was swinging back and forth, making a sound like a rusty hinge. The other children started clapping. They said Toby was "winning." I can't get them to stop clapping. Their hands are starting to bleed.
