Day 14: The Growth Spurt

I found Sarah, the quietest girl in class, in the nurse's office. She’s grown six feet since yesterday. Her limbs are thin as wires, and she’s folded herself into a medicine cabinet. She told me she can hear the "sun screaming." She says the atmosphere is turning into a giant ear, and soon it will hear everything we think. I have a scalpel. I’m thinking about my own ears.
